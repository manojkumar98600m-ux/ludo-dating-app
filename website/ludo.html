How to play

Roll a 6 to bring a token out. Tap one of your highlighted tokens to move it. Rolling a 6 gives another turn. Land on an opponent to send them home. First player to finish all four tokens wins.

This version is pass-and-play on one device. Online rooms, accounts and matchmaking need a secure realtime server and are not active yet.
